WHAT WE DO

Protection Services

Every post we take is a covenant of care. From the sanctuary to the C-suite, we provide elite armed protection — always engaged, always ready.

🏢
01

Corporate Security

Protecting People, Assets & Culture.

Your business is more than a building — it's your people, your assets, and your reputation. Our corporate security services are designed to protect all three, without disrupting the professional environment you've built. Our agents dress and present themselves as members of your team — professional, polished, and engaged — while maintaining the tactical readiness to respond to any incident.

🏗

In-Office Security Presence

Our agents operate within your office environment in professional attire, providing a consistent protective presence at reception, common areas, server rooms, and executive floors.

👤

Individual 1:1 Protection

For specific employees facing threats — whether from a disgruntled former employee, a stalker, or a high-stakes business dispute — we provide a dedicated agent for as long as the threat is active. We are also available to provide a secure, professional presence during employee terminations when elevated risk is a concern.

🌲

Corporate Retreat Security

Off-site retreats, team-building events, and leadership gatherings at remote or unfamiliar venues require dedicated security planning. We assess locations in advance, embed within the event, and provide round-the-clock protective coverage.

🚗

Mobile Roving Patrols

Regular patrols of your corporate campus, parking structures, and perimeter areas — deterring criminal activity, identifying vulnerabilities, and maintaining a visible security presence without a heavy footprint.

💼

Secure Asset Transportation

High-value physical assets, sensitive documents, equipment, and cash transfers require a security escort from origin to destination. Our agents provide armed escort and chain-of-custody documentation throughout the entire transport process.

🎪

Special Event Security

Product launches, corporate dinners, investor events, and large employee gatherings each carry unique risk profiles. We develop a security plan tailored to your event's size, location, and guest list — then staff it accordingly.

🛡
02

Executive Security

Discreet. Decisive. Always Present.

High-profile individuals carry high-profile risk. Executives, CEOs, ministry leaders, VIPs, dignitaries, and public figures face threats that most people never encounter. Our executive protection details are built around one principle: your safety is never compromised, and your schedule never stops. We work around you, not the other way around.

👔

Personal Security Detail (PSD)

A dedicated close-protection agent or full detail team assigned to you or your principal — operating in concealment for maximum discretion. We move with you, adapt with you, and protect you at every step.

🗺

Advance Work & Route Planning

Before you arrive anywhere, we're already there. Our agents conduct advance surveys of venues, hotels, event spaces, and travel corridors — identifying vulnerabilities, mapping exits, and coordinating with local contacts so every move is calculated.

🚘

Transportation & Travel Security

Coordinated ground transportation with trained protective drivers, escort vehicles, and dedicated travel security agents. From pick-ups to multi-stop itineraries and destination coverage through hotels, meetings, and public appearances — we manage every transition to eliminate exposure and keep your schedule moving.

🎤

Event & Public Appearance Security

Galas, speaking engagements, media appearances, award ceremonies, and public-facing events require careful security choreography. We integrate with event staff, control access points, and maintain a protective perimeter — invisibly.

⚠

Threat Intelligence & Assessment

We work with you to identify and evaluate specific threats — including disgruntled individuals, digital threats, and reputational risks — and develop a proactive response plan before a situation escalates.

📞

24/7 Emergency Response

Threats don't keep business hours. Our clients have direct access to their assigned agent or on-call team leader around the clock — for emergencies, changes in threat level, or sudden itinerary shifts that require immediate response.

🎯
03

Special Events & Venue Security

Command the Crowd. Control the Environment. Protect the Experience.

Large-scale events bring large-scale responsibility. Whether you're hosting thousands at a concert, a festival, a sporting event, or a community gathering, the stakes are high and the variables are many. Our event security teams are trained to manage high-density crowds, coordinate with local law enforcement, and respond decisively to any threat — all while preserving the atmosphere your guests came to enjoy. We don't just stand at a gate. We own the environment.

🎤

Concert & Festival Security

From general admission floor coverage to backstage access control, our agents are deployed strategically throughout your venue — managing crowd flow, identifying threats early, and responding instantly when incidents arise.

🏟

Venue & Perimeter Control

We establish and maintain a secure perimeter around your event footprint — managing entry and exit points, screening for prohibited items, and deterring unauthorized access from the moment gates open to the last car out of the parking lot.

👥

Crowd Management

Our agents are trained in crowd dynamics and de-escalation techniques — positioning themselves to prevent surges, manage bottlenecks, and diffuse conflicts before they escalate into incidents requiring force.

🎭

Artist & VIP Protection

Performers, headliners, promoters, and event VIPs require dedicated close protection. We provide personal security details that integrate with your production team, managing green room access, stage transitions, and post-show egress.

🚨

Emergency Response Coordination

We develop and rehearse emergency action plans for every event — coordinating with local law enforcement, fire, and EMS. When seconds matter, our team already knows exactly what to do and who to call.

📋

Pre-Event Security Planning

Every successful event security operation starts weeks before gates open. We conduct site walks, review venue layouts, assess threat profiles, build staffing plans, and brief every agent on assignment-specific protocols before day one.

✝
04

House of Worship Security

Protecting Sacred Spaces. Honoring Holy Ground.

Your congregation gathers to encounter God — not to worry about their safety. Our agents integrate seamlessly into your church culture, operating in concealment so that your members feel welcome, not watched. We become part of your community while maintaining an unwavering protective posture. No detail is too small. No risk is taken lightly.

📋

Security Consultations

A comprehensive assessment of your facility, staff, and existing protocols — followed by a tailored security plan built around your congregation's specific needs and culture.

🏫

Security Team Training

Want to build your own volunteer safety team? We train your people — from situational awareness and de-escalation to emergency response and active threat protocols. We equip your team to serve.

🛡

Contract Armed Protection

Our licensed, concealed-carry operators attend your services fully armed and fully engaged — providing visible community presence and invisible protective coverage during every service.

🚗

Mobile Roving Patrols

Mobile units conduct continuous perimeter patrols around your worship location — covering parking lots, entrances, and surrounding areas before, during, and after services.

🎤

Conference & Event Security

Large worship events, multi-day conferences, revivals, and special services require elevated security postures. We scale our team to your event size and coordinate seamlessly with your event staff.

⛺

Camp & Retreat Security

Youth camps, adult retreats, and off-site ministry gatherings present unique security challenges. Our agents embed within the program, providing 24-hour protective coverage in remote or outdoor environments.

👤

Individual 1:1 Protection

Pastoral leaders, guest speakers, and ministry VIPs sometimes require a dedicated personal security detail. Our 1:1 protection service provides a discreet, professional agent assigned exclusively to one individual.

🔍

Threat & Vulnerability Assessment

A thorough review of your facility's physical security, access control, camera coverage, emergency exits, and staff awareness — resulting in a written risk report and actionable recommendations.

WHO WE ARE

Why Hire
Harp & Spear

Harp & Spear draws its name from King David — a man of profound worship and unmatched courage on the battlefield. Before he was a king, David was a shepherd who wrote psalms of deep devotion to God. Yet when called upon, that same David stepped onto the battlefield and became Israel's greatest military commander. He didn't choose between faith and force. He held both — with equal conviction and unwavering purpose.

That dual calling is the foundation of everything we do. We operate with what's known as the sheepdog mindset. A sheepdog lives among the flock — calm, engaged, and familiar. It moves alongside the people it protects, earning trust simply through its presence. But it carries the same capacity for decisive action as the wolf, and when the moment demands it, it does not hesitate.

That is who we are. Our agents blend naturally into your environment — your Sunday service, your executive suite, your corporate event. We are approachable, discreet, and fully present. And when the moment calls for it, we are ready to act with absolute resolve.
