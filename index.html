OUR MISSION

The Worshiper.
The Warrior.

Harp & Spear Consulting draws its name and identity from King David — the shepherd boy who became Israel's greatest king. David was a man of profound faith who wrote psalms of worship, and a warrior of unmatched courage who defended his people without hesitation.

We carry that same dual calling into our work. Our operators are men and women of faith who are also highly trained security professionals — ready to worship in the sanctuary and stand watch at the gate.

Based in Colorado Springs, Colorado, we serve churches, corporations, and individuals who require elite protection delivered with integrity, discretion, and a moral compass grounded in biblical values.
